**Reflection 2 (prediction, written before looking at any data).** I expect CO2 in
Le Mans to be *rising* over the last 90 days. Over this window the growing season
ends: leaves fall and plants stop absorbing CO2 through photosynthesis, so the
natural summer "sink" weakens. At the same time, human emissions increase after the
summer break (back-to-school and return to work bring more traffic) and heating
starts as temperatures drop. Both effects push the concentration up.
